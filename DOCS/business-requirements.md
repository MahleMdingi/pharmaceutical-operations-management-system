# Business Requirements

## Project Overview

The Pharmaceutical Operations Management System is a SQL Server database project designed to simulate real-world pharmaceutical business operations and backend workflows.

The system focuses on managing:

* pharmaceutical products
* manufacturing batches
* suppliers
* warehouses
* inventory
* customer orders
* shipments

The goal of the project is to design a scalable relational database capable of supporting operational and transactional business processes within a pharmaceutical environment.

---

# Business Problems

The pharmaceutical company currently faces several operational challenges, including:

* Poor inventory tracking
* Difficulty monitoring medication batches
* Lack of centralized supplier management
* Inefficient order tracking
* Limited shipment visibility
* Expiry monitoring challenges

The company requires a centralized system to improve operational efficiency and data management.

---

# Main Business Processes

## 1. Product Management

The system stores pharmaceutical product information including:

* product names
* dosage information
* pricing
* product categories

---

## 2. Batch Tracking

Each medication batch must track:

* batch numbers
* manufacturing dates
* expiry dates
* quantities produced

---

## 3. Supplier Management

The system manages suppliers responsible for providing pharmaceutical products.

---

## 4. Inventory Management

The system tracks stock quantities stored in warehouses.

---

## 5. Order Processing

Customers such as pharmacies and hospitals place orders for pharmaceutical products.

---

## 6. Shipment Tracking

The system tracks shipment information and delivery statuses for customer orders.

---

# System Goals

The database system aims to:

* centralize operational data
* improve inventory tracking
* support transactional workflows
* reduce data redundancy
* improve reporting capabilities
* simulate real-world backend database operations
